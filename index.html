Our story

A promise kept, one family at a time.

Sri Vishnu Insurance Consultancy opened its doors on Market Street, Perambur, in 1993. Three decades later, we serve the children — and grandchildren — of the families who first trusted us.

We believe insurance is not a product to be sold, but a promise to be kept. That is why we stand beside every client through claims, renewals and life’s milestones — and why advice here is always free, in Tamil or English.
